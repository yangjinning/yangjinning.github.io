I am a PhD student in Data Science at City University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Xiangyu Zhao. I received my master's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, and previously studied Finance at Jinan University(暨南大学), supervised by Professor Jinmin Du(杜金岷).

My research interests include:

- Multimodal Large Language Models, Multimodal Representation Learning
- LLM-driven Agents for Financial Trading
- LLMs for Science, Healthcare, and Finance

### HeartLLM: Discretized ECG Tokenization for LLM-Based Diagnostic Reasoning.

My research develops discretized tokenization methods that convert time-series physiological signals into discrete semantic tokens for multimodal large language models, enabling clinical question answering and report generation.

Jinning Yang, Wenjie Sun, Wen Shi. AAAI 2026.
